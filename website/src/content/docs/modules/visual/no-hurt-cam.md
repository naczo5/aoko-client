> **Beta.** This module lives in the Beta tab of the external GUI. It was not thoroughly tested; use it at your own risk.

*No Hurt Cam* removes or stabilizes the screen shaking and tilting animation triggered when the player takes damage, providing a clean and steady view during intense PvP.

## Version support

1.8.9 · 1.21.x · 26.1 · 26.2 · 26.3

## Usage notes

- Keeps crosshair orientation stable when taking damage from arrows, fire, poison, or melee hits.
- Client-side visual only with no impact on server-side health or invulnerability ticks.
