> **Beta.** This module lives in the Beta tab of the external GUI. It was not thoroughly tested; use it at your own risk.

*TNT Timer* renders the remaining fuse time in seconds above primed TNT entities in your render distance, giving you precise timing to avoid explosions or time TNT jumps.

## Version support

1.8.9 · 1.21.x · 26.1 · 26.2 · 26.3

## Usage notes

- Renders a clean countdown tag above ticking TNT blocks.
- Works across Bedwars, Skywars, and vanilla game modes.
