title: Break Progress

---

> **Beta.** This module lives in the Beta tab of the external GUI. It was not thoroughly tested; use it at your own risk.

*Break Progress* shows a percentage over every block that is being mined, including blocks other players are breaking, such as your bed in Bedwars.

## Version support

1.8.9 · 1.21.x · 26.1 · 26.2 · 26.3

## Usage notes

- Your own mining shows the exact progress. Other players' progress comes from the crack stage the server sends, in 10% steps.
- When the breaker is a player, their name appears under the percentage.
