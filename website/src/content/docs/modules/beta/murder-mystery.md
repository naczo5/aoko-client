title: Murder Mystery

> **Beta.** This module lives in the Beta tab of the external GUI. It was not thoroughly tested; use it at your own risk.

*Murder Mystery* watches what other players hold. Anyone seen holding a murder weapon is tagged **MURDERER** for the rest of the round, and bow holders are tagged **BOW**. Known murderers are also listed near the top of the screen with their distance.

## Version support

1.8.9 · 1.21.x · 26.1 · 26.2 · 26.3

## Usage notes

- The weapon list follows Raven bS's default Murder Mystery knife skins.
- The list of murderers resets when you change worlds.
- Turn it off in other game modes: any sword holder counts as a murderer.
