> **Beta.** This module lives in the Beta tab of the external GUI. It was not thoroughly tested; use it at your own risk.

*WTap* automatically resets player sprint upon landing attacks to maximize outgoing knockback and maintain spacing during combat combos.

## Version support

1.8.9 · 1.21.x · 26.1 · 26.2

## Usage notes

- Engages while attacking an opponent, briefly resetting the sprint state to deal first-hit sprint knockback repeatedly.
- Can be bound to a custom key in the External GUI.
